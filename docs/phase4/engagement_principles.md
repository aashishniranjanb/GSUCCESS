# Phase 4 Engagement Principles & Ethical Product Guidelines

**GPAT 2027 Learning OS**  
**Core Directive:** Optimize for exam readiness, retained knowledge, and learner well-being — not infinite attention capture or compulsive daily active use.

---

## 1. Ethical Engagement Principles

### Principle 1: Eliminate Decision Fatigue (The One-Action Daily Mission)
- **Problem:** When opening an exam prep app, learners are typically presented with a confusing dashboard of 20 subjects, analytics graphs, and menus, prompting hesitation and distraction.
- **Solution:** Display one clear starting card: *"Today's 15-Minute Study Mission"*, stating the estimated time, the composition (e.g. 2 overdue reviews, 1 misconception repair, 2 high-yield new questions), and the concrete learning objective.

### Principle 2: Competence Over Compulsion (Visible Mastery Map)
- **Problem:** Extrinsic gamification (streaks, points, badges) makes learners feel anxious when life interrupts study and encourages superficial "easy question" grinding.
- **Solution:** Replace streak anxiety with a **Syllabus Mastery Map**. Progress reflects verified delayed recall and error repair across 5 states:
  1. `UNASSESSED` (Untested syllabus territory)
  2. `LEARNING` (Initial encounters)
  3. `NEEDS_REPAIR` (Active misconception or careless error)
  4. `RETAINING` (Under active FSRS spaced review schedule)
  5. `DEMONSTRATED_MASTERY` (Successful recall after spaced intervals)

### Principle 3: Make Learning Itself Rewarding (Diagnostic Active Recall)
- **Problem:** Generic flashcard apps give binary correct/wrong feedback without explaining the underlying cognitive trap.
- **Solution:** Feedback identifies the exact distractor trap (e.g. *"Prazosin lowers peripheral resistance; it does not trigger hypertensive crisis"*), links to the source textbook page, and schedules an immediate or delayed repair challenge.

### Principle 4: Graceful Resumption (No Guilt, No Punitive Streaks)
- **Problem:** Missing a day resets a 30-day streak to 0, demotivating learners who experience burnout or exam fatigue.
- **Solution:** Never destroy earned milestones. When a learner returns after an absence, greet them with an adaptive catch-up plan rather than red warning banners. Spaced repetition cards are gracefully rescheduled by memory retrievability.

### Principle 5: Digital Well-Being & Learner Autonomy
- Provide clear quiet-hours controls (no notification between 10 PM and 7 AM).
- Support reduced-motion preferences (`prefers-reduced-motion`) for accessible, distraction-free studying.
- Zero infinite scroll feeds; every mission has an explicit finish line.

## 2. Explicit Anti-Patterns (Features We Will NOT Build)

| Prohibited Feature | Why It Is Harmful to Competitive Exam Preparation |
|---|---|
| **Infinite Question Scrolling** | Promotes passive recognition rather than deliberate retrieval; drains study stamina. |
| **Random Loot / Variable Reward Schedules** | Exploits dopamine loops without improving retention or understanding. |
| **Punitive Streak Freezes / Loss Threats** | Induces guilt and anxiety; causes abandonment after a missed day. |
| **Leaderboards Ranked by Screen Time** | Rewards unproductive browsing and guessing rather than mastery. |
| **Unsubstantiated Mastery Claims** | Inflates learner confidence falsely before a high-stakes national exam. |
| **Intrusive Late-Night Push Alerts** | Disrupts sleep, which is physiologically essential for memory consolidation. |
